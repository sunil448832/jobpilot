\subsection{LLM Fine-tuning \& Evaluation Pipeline – Vision-Language Models (Freelance) \hfill \href{https://huggingface.co/sunil448832/blip-model}{HuggingFace Model}}
\begin{zitemize}
\item Fine-tuned \textbf{LLaVA 1.6 (Mistral-7B)} with \textbf{LoRA} (r=128, $\alpha$=256) under \textbf{DeepSpeed ZeRO-3} --- \textbf{bf16 / tf32} mixed precision, gradient checkpointing, cosine LR schedule --- over a curated \textbf{2{,}000-pair} instruction dataset (1{,}600 / 400 train/test split).
\item Designed and ran the surrounding \textbf{data preparation, training, and evaluation pipelines} with \textbf{Weights \& Biases} experiment tracking and best-checkpoint selection on evaluation loss; published the fine-tuned \textbf{BLIP} model to the \textbf{HuggingFace Hub}.
\item Benchmarked three recipes head-to-head --- LoRA-tuned \textbf{LLaVA}, a fine-tuned \textbf{BLIP} baseline (HuggingFace \texttt{Trainer}), and \textbf{CLIP} nearest-neighbour retrieval --- to select the best-performing approach.
\item \textbf{Stack:} PyTorch, HuggingFace Transformers, LLaVA, DeepSpeed ZeRO-3, LoRA, CLIP, BLIP, W\&B, CUDA, flash-attention.
\end{zitemize}
