\subsection{RL Post-Training of Qwen3-4B for Competition Math (GRPO + Verifiable Rewards, Personal) \hfill \href{https://github.com/sunil448832/preference-alignment-grpo}{GitHub Repository}}
\begin{zitemize}
\item Implemented \textbf{GRPO} with verifiable rewards from scratch in \textbf{PyTorch} (group-relative advantages, clipped policy gradient, tempered log-probs); \textbf{QLoRA} (NF4, r=16) on \textbf{Qwen3-4B-Instruct}, \textbf{math-verify} reward over 5K competition problems.
\item RL systems layer: \textbf{colocated vLLM} rollouts with per-step \textbf{LoRA hot-swap} and sleep mode on a single 48\,GB GPU, adaptive resampling of zero-signal prompts (gradient-carrying groups 17\% to 42\%), token-budget micro-batching, checkpoint resume.
\item On 500 held-out problems after 280 steps (\textasciitilde1 GPU-day): pass rate \textbf{38.6\% to 41.0\%}, solutions \textbf{16\% shorter}, truncations 33\% to 21\%. \textbf{Stack:} PyTorch, vLLM, PEFT, bitsandbytes, HuggingFace Transformers, AWS SageMaker.
\end{zitemize}
