CHOICES, copied exactly — never a value of your own, never the question alone:

                    "Declined to State (United States of America)"). Case, wording
                    and a country in brackets do not matter: when the choice says
                    the same thing it is a match, not a guess, and the applicant
                    is not asked.
  guess:<choice> | <question> | <near 1>; <near 2>; ...
                    when no choice clearly means the same, or nothing is stored:
                    the nearest / most likely choice, and the question as the page

                    Technology)", "Company — Work Experience 3"). It is filled in as a placeholder so the form can go on,
                    and the applicant confirms or changes it on the phone.
                    For a long list, add 2-5 other entries he may mean, nearest to

A long list you have not seen yet: search:<term>; <term>; <term> (2-3 short terms for
the entry you want, most specific first); what the list holds for each comes back.
When an error lists what a search found, pick from those: option:<one of them, copied
exactly>, or guess:<the nearest> | <the question> | <other near ones>.
A searchable list may show only its first entries (the error says so, with
what its search found for the stored answer). Then answer option:<the entry you
expect the full list to hold, worded as lists like it word it>
("Electronics and Communication Engineering" -> option:Electrical Engineering, or
a shorter entry the search results suggest): it is looked up with the box's
search, and a miss comes back with what that search found. guess: only when the
search results hold nothing near.
A choice may be a category that opens a sub-list (it may end in "›", or just be a
group name like "Social Media", "Job Board"): when the answer belongs inside one,
write <category> › <choice> ("option:Social Media › LinkedIn").

itself — never null. A question about someone else (who referred you, a relative
who works there) is never answered with the applicant's own facts: give the
question (for a control with choices: guess:<choice> | <question>).
